Tab 01 · End-to-End Agent Evaluation & Trajectory Benchmarking Pipelines

The MTM sweep's first category is the "CI/CD for agent performance" primitive — a system that automatically evaluates an agent's entire sequence of actions (the trajectory) against a goal, judging not just final output but the quality of reasoning, tool choice, error recovery, and efficiency.
